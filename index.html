ABOUT THIS LAB

A Galton board turns independent random choices into a visible distribution. With n rows, each ball’s final bin follows a binomial distribution—and as n grows, that shape approaches a normal curve.

P(X = k) = ⁿCₖ pᵏ (1 − p)ⁿ⁻ᵏ

A 30-SECOND GUIDE

How the board works

At every peg, a ball gets one chance to turn left or right. After n rows, its bin number equals the number of right turns it made.

X ∼ Binomial(n, p)

Try a few runs with the same settings. A small sample can be uneven; as the sample grows, the observed bars tend to follow the theoretical curve.
